### C6. Dual-coefficient set-native theorem and continuous cap certificate

**Materialized object.** The protocol and direct-child artifact tags are

**Result and value.** Completing empty sets to `{0,1}`, both coefficients use
only the binary set:

\[
q_i=\max S_i^+,
\qquad
w_i=\min_{y\in S_i^+}\{(1-y)r_i-y\,\mathrm{LGD}\}.
\]

Under exact budget, no cash, bounded loans, an exhaustive disjoint purpose
partition with upper caps only, nonnegative rates, positive LGD, and feasible
full-budget singleton-zero support, strict substitution proves that every
maximin optimizer uses singleton-zero loans. The complete optimal face and
  value are identical for every constructed set-risk cap in `[0,1]`. All 208
  primary CatBoost--Platt menus, 88 development and 120 primary OOT, certify the assumptions by
reconciling the 1,248 hash-pinned predecessor witnesses. No new LP was solved.

**Decision changed.** The valuable continuous-cap result was obtained by
theory and logical certificates, not by extending the seven-hour grid. It also
shows that fully set-native coefficients can collapse the economic frontier
rather than produce a rich robust tradeoff.

**Stop rule.** This is conditional structural mathematics, not true zero risk,
joint coverage, a unique optimizer, selected-set validity, or a preferred
policy. Do not rerun caps inside this model; the face is already proven constant.

## 6. Designed work that needs fresh data, authority, or theory

### D0. Fresh temporal origin and theorem-compatible transport contract

**Question.** What exact coverage or decision-loss event should transport to
one future loan or one future issue-month block?

**Existing design.** The complete two-track contract is
[`ijds_temporal_transport_dependence_contract_2026-07-30.md`](ijds_temporal_transport_dependence_contract_2026-07-30.md):

- Track A targets marginal coverage of one future loan and requires a valid
  swap-distance, decoupling, or other theorem-compatible penalty;
- Track B uses one complete issue month as the context and targets a declared
  block loss.

**Minimum new input.** A genuinely later untouched cohort or an external source
with fixed population, point-in-time features, label-availability chronology,
complete role assignment, and an independently justified probability-law
bridge.

Feedback-covariate-shift theory is eligible only if the future algorithm's
query/action law and its conformal weights are specified and evaluable. A
historical allocation or optimized dollar exposure cannot be relabeled as that
law after outcomes.

**Value.** High and load-bearing. D1 and D3 cannot become inferential studies
without it.

**Decision rule.** Choose the loan or block estimand before target outcomes.
The two tracks may not be merged into one headline.

**Stop rule.** Stop if a required discrepancy/decoupling/mixing upper bound is
unavailable, postselected, or vacuous; if context chronology is incomplete; or
if current PSI/KS/rolling diagnostics are offered as substitutes.

### D1. Equal-notional fixed-(K) JOMI

**Question.** Can selection-conditional coverage be obtained for the funded
loans while making count FCP and invested-dollar FCP identical?

**Existing design.** Phase 1 of
[`ijds_selected_exposure_validity_design_2026-07-30.md`](ijds_selected_exposure_validity_design_2026-07-30.md)
freezes one learner, exact (K), equal exposure (B/K), a deterministic
permutation-equivariant selector, disjoint policy-construction and final JOMI
calibration roles, complete administrative outcomes, and complete swap-specific reference
sets.

**Why first.** It targets the paper's central unclosed handoff directly and
removes the count-versus-dollar ambiguity by design:

\[
\operatorname{FCP}^{\$}=\operatorname{FCP}^{N}.
\]

**Minimum new input.** Same-population exchangeable or theorem-justified fresh
calibration/target units, plus an outcome-free reference-set feasibility pilot.

**Stop rule.** Stop before outcomes if any required reference set is empty or
cannot attain the declared rank, permutations or ID reversal change selection,
the support is not exactly (K), administrative outcomes are unresolved, or final
calibration labels affect policy construction.

### D2. Concentration-bounded fractional exposure

**Question.** Can a valid count-FCR construction conservatively control
expected dollar-weighted FCP when allocations are unequal?

**Design.** After D1, freeze (kappa\ge1) and enforce

\[
\frac{a_i}{A}\le\frac{\kappa}{K},
\qquad
\operatorname{FCP}^{\$}\le\kappa\operatorname{FCP}^{N}.
\]

Run JOMI at (alpha/\kappa). This is an indirect deterministic reduction,
not direct exposure-weighted conformal validity.

**Value.** High only after D1 succeeds; it reconnects statistical control to a
less artificial allocation.

**Stop rule.** Stop if (kappa) is tuned on outcomes, smaller nominal error
makes sets uninformative, cash makes a (B)-relative bound inapplicable to the
invested-dollar denominator, or the concentration inequality is not enforced
for every realization.

### D3. Context-level decision-loss control by CRC or LTT

**Question.** Can a frozen end-to-end policy control expected fixed-capital
monthly miscoverage loss

\[
L_t(\pi)=\frac1B\sum_i a_{it}^{\pi}
\mathbf1\{Y_{it}\notin C_{it}^{\pi}\}
\]

on a new complete issue-month context?

**Existing design.** The four-way train/design/risk-calibration/evaluation
contract and all theorem routes are in
[`ijds_decision_loss_aligned_conformal_design_2026-07-30.md`](ijds_decision_loss_aligned_conformal_design_2026-07-30.md).

**Route decision before outcomes.** Use:

1. classical CRC only for a universally pointwise-monotone nested-set family,
   preferably with allocation fixed across the calibration parameter;
2. stability-based CRC only with exchangeability, symmetry, a full-data
   reference algorithm, and a valid nonvacuous stability remainder;
3. finite-grid non-monotone CRC only with i.i.d. bounded context losses and a
   nonnegative attainable working level after the exact excess term; or
4. LTT for a frozen finite reoptimized catalog with valid bounded-loss p-values
   and predeclared FWER control.

Conformal Policy Control is not a fifth interchangeable retrofit. It changes
the object to the expected risk of a stochastic policy interpolated with a
predeclared safe policy. Pursue it only under a separate protocol with bounded
loss, mutual absolute continuity, a uniform density floor, Lipschitz
regularity, replace-one stability, exact policy-ratio conformal weights, and
immediate complete feedback; otherwise the $\alpha+K\epsilon/\delta$ bound is
unavailable.

For finite-grid CRC,

\[
D(m,n)=\sqrt{\frac{\log(2m)}{2n}}
+\frac{1}{2\sqrt{2n\log(2m)}}
\]

for the unit-bounded loss. With (m=2), (D(2,11)=0.341563),
(D(2,15)=0.292497), and (n=129) is the first integer below 0.10. The
current monthly counts cannot support a nonvacuous 0.10 retrofit.

**Value.** Very high, but it answers aggregate monthly risk rather than focal
selected-loan coverage. It is an alternative scientific target to D1, not a
stronger synonym.

**Stop rule.** Stop if contexts are not defensibly i.i.d./exchangeable under the
chosen theorem, any administrative outcome is incomplete, a policy-context loss cell is
missing, monotonicity or stability fails, the finite-grid correction is
vacuous, no nontrivial policy is certified, or CRC is switched to LTT after
outcomes without a new protocol.

### D4. Independent calibrator selection and recalibration

**Question.** If a calibrator must be selected, does an independently selected
and then independently recalibrated map preserve its coverage/efficiency state
and decision relevance on a new target?

**Minimum design.** Four disjoint roles: base-model fitting; method selection;
final recalibration/conformal fitting; and untouched evaluation. Fix the
selection loss, candidate maps, scalarization for Venn, taxonomy, and whether
the selected map enters the optimizer before evaluation.

**Value.** Medium. C2 already answers the nonselective retrospective sensitivity
and shows that no family-uniform shortfall exists. D4 is warranted only if
calibrator selection itself becomes the scientific question.

**Stop rule.** Stop if the same outcomes select and evaluate the map, if a Venn
pair is silently replaced by a favorable scalar, if method-specific taxonomies
change the estimand, or if a portfolio conclusion is made without propagating
the frozen selected map through a separately locked optimizer.

### D5. Honest external V2

**Question.** Does the descriptive binary-geometry frontier recur under another
compatible archive and chronology?

**Minimum design.** A post-inspection protocol, exact source/version hashes,
configurable roots, truly ordered fit/calibration/target blocks, point-in-time
features, a status-independent universe, a declared endpoint and maturity
contract, frozen target-independent recipes, complete target sets/support/
coverage, unresolved-outcome handling, and exact artifact transport.

**Value.** Medium-high as secondary evidence after D0, but it transfers no
portfolio or coverage guarantee to Lending Club.

**Stop rule.** Stop if chronology is only a random train/test split, source
identity or acquisition authority is unverified, the endpoint is a selected
resolved snapshot, target support and sets are not evaluated, or the result
precedes its protocol. Legal redistribution and release packaging remain F3.

### D6. A genuinely constraint-native or jointly defined uncertainty model

**Question.** Can a future optimizer use a directly defined multivariate or
constraint-native uncertainty object whose theorem matches its actual
feasibility or decision loss?

**What is already closed.** C5 makes only risk set-native; C6 makes both the
risk and standardized-payoff coefficients binary-set-native and proves the
resulting cap frontier collapses under the inherited polytope.

**What remains new.** A method in the spirit of conformal uncertainty sets,
predict-then-calibrate contextual LP, SPO-RC, C-MICL, or decision-calibrated
catalogs would define a different joint/contextual object rather than embed the
old marginal binary sets.

**Value.** Potentially high, but methodologically larger than the current
paper. It should become a separate protocol only after the uncertain object,
joint law, loss, and theorem are explicit.

**Stop rule.** Stop if marginal sets are simply multiplied into a claimed
joint region, if a conditional-independence or exchangeability assumption is
borrowed rather than audited, or if a new formulation is chosen by retrospective
outcome direction.

### D7. Exact adjustability or continuous optimal-face analysis for a new model

**Question.** Does an exact continuous parameter or adjustability result change
the identification conclusion for a clearly specified future optimization
model?

**Value.** Low for the existing LP after C6; potentially useful for a new model
whose optimal-face structure is itself the scientific object.

**Minimum method.** Exact or certified parametric analysis, one-sided dual
conditions at seams, explicit degeneracy semantics, complete affine-hull and
normal-cone treatment, and a proof that the computed domain is exhaustive.

**Stop rule.** Do not use dense floating-point caps, solver basis ranges, or one
common returned vertex as equality of optimal faces or uniqueness.

**Question.** If the paper later makes an economic-return claim, what cash-flow,

**Value.** High only if the intended contribution changes from standardized

## 8. How the paper evolved from CatBoost + Venn--Abers + Mondrian + portfolio

### 8.1 The original pipeline was reasonable as engineering

The early design was coherent in the ordinary ML sense:

1. fit a strong CatBoost credit-risk model;
2. compare Platt, isotonic, and Venn--Abers probability calibration and select
   Venn--Abers on calibration diagnostics;
3. use score-Mondrian binary residual intervals to represent heterogeneous
   uncertainty;
4. pass a conservative continuous upper endpoint to a robust LP; and
5. choose and report an economically attractive policy.

Each component has legitimate literature. Venn--Abers was and remains a valid
calibration construction under its own contract. Mondrian grouping is a
reasonable heterogeneity device. Upper-bound robust optimization is a standard
design pattern. Outcome-free policy construction is better than selecting
directly on final outcomes.

What was missing was a proof that the guarantee survived **all** interfaces.
The original work treated engineering composability as inferential
composability.

### 8.2 Why each transfer was later rejected or narrowed

| Old step | Why it once looked acceptable | What the audit found | Current treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| CatBoost champion | Strong historical predictive metrics and a familiar model-selection workflow | Population, calendar, endpoint, availability, and inclusion rules changed; legacy and active metrics are not a valid horse race | CatBoost/Platt is the frozen primary path; four additional learners are coverage controls and no OOT model wins |
| Selected Venn--Abers scalar | Venn--Abers is calibrated and the scalar was convenient downstream | Venn's guarantee belongs to its multiprobability/set-valued object, not automatically to one scalar, residual interval, temporal target, or funded set | Scalar IVAP is one declared member of a complete four-map sensitivity; Venn itself is not refuted or selected |
| Score-Mondrian residuals | Local grouping can reduce heterogeneity and is standard conformal practice | Score strata do not create temporal exchangeability or label-conditional validity; exact binary geometry can change sharply at finite ranks | Five groups remain protocol-fixed; label-Mondrian is a complete descriptive sensitivity, not a repair |
| Continuous interval upper endpoint | A conservative upper number is easy to use as LP risk | Binary coverage identifies endpoint contact with labels zero/one, not the interior continuous magnitude; set-preserving embeddings can change allocations | The chosen embedding is declared, V1d audits the complete embedding family, and C4 gives the exact invariance criterion |
| Robust LP cap | A larger upper coefficient appears conservative | Same numeric caps under different score units define different feasible sets; neither candidate coverage nor marginal sets give funded-set or joint-product validity | Two rulers expose comparator dependence; named/registered supports are finite diagnostics and no cap/ruler wins |
| Resolved-only OOT evaluation | Standard supervised metrics use observed labels | Conditioning on administrative resolution changes the candidate population and leaks maturity/status information | Candidate membership is status-independent; 12,076 unresolved outcomes remain under sharp common-completion bounds |
| Selected policy and payoff headline | Policy search and OOT comparison are common ML practice | Multiple inspected axes, outcome reuse, comparator mismatch, and an ambiguous payoff proxy invalidated superiority and economic-return claims | No learner, calibrator, gamma, ruler, coordinate, scenario, comparator, or policy is selected; standardized payoff is named exactly |
| “Set-native robust” escape | Using the worst binary label avoids an arbitrary continuous endpoint | Marginal sets are not jointly covered; empty-set handling is normative; a binary coefficient is coarse; risk-only GO2 still used point-score payoff | GO2 is a complete challenger audit; C6 makes both coefficients set-native and proves the inherited cap frontier collapses |

### 8.3 Why the current paper is better

The current paper is better because its claims are narrower but logically
connected:

- the eligible population is status-independent and exhaustive under the
  declared contract;
- feature, label, calibration, policy, and endpoint clocks are physically
  separated;
- unresolved outcomes remain visible through sharp partial identification;
- probability calibration, residual calibration, candidate coverage,
  selected coverage, exposure weighting, and decision calibration are named as
  different objects;
- exact binary geometry replaces informal continuity or phase stories;
- comparator units and opportunity costs are explicit through two rulers;
- binary-set preservation is separated from continuous embedding and global
  decision invariance;
- complete grids report unfavorable, null, and mixed results without choosing
  a winner;
- set-native alternatives are evaluated and bounded rather than advertised as
  automatic validity repairs; and
- every retained number has an explicit lineage and forbidden-inference
  boundary.

It is also limited in a more honest way. It supplies no prospective temporal
guarantee, selected/funded-set validity, joint product-set coverage,
probabilistic robust-optimization theorem, policy winner, causal effect, or
cash-flow return. “Better” means more valid and auditable, not more favorable.

### 8.4 Why future revisions may still retire current material

Current claims are not protected by confidence or recency. A future theorem,
fresh cohort, endpoint, or independently calibrated decision object may make
some current diagnostics secondary. The rule is the same one that improved
the old paper: retain a statement while its premises, estimand, lineage, and
decision relevance remain valid; narrow or retire it when an adversarial audit
shows otherwise.

## 10. Universal stop rules for any successor

Every successor expected to exceed 30 minutes or use new outcomes must satisfy
all of the following before execution:

1. one exact estimand, sampling unit, population, and theorem;
2. immutable train/design/calibration/evaluation roles and chronology;

   complete reporting denominator;
4. no target outcome in learner, calibrator, taxonomy, policy, comparator,

   multiplicity;

7. synthetic positive controls and adversarial negative controls;

   cancellation, and resume rules;
9. canonical complete-grid consolidation and exact artifact transport; and
10. a predeclared rule for null, unfavorable, empty, infeasible, timeout, and

If one gate fails, stop and report the failure. Never shrink a grid, extend a
deadline, switch a theorem, change a loss, select a cell, or reuse opened
